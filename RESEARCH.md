# Research: what makes a student portfolio stand out

## Round 1 — what makes a site look AI-made

Studies of AI-built pages (buildtolaunch.substack.com — 120 pages; dev.to/laurilllll; noqode.fr; interfacekit.io; regards.design)
found the same defaults again and again: icon-on-top feature cards, one icon set (Lucide), default shadcn colours, Inter/Geist,
a pill badge above a centred headline, one gradient word, every section fading up on scroll, tiny uppercase labels, purple
gradients and glowing blobs, and copy that could fit anyone. This site avoids all of them.

## Round 2 — the most-liked personal sites

I went through wonderfulsite.org — 789 personal sites shared under @zarazhangrui's "your personal website is the new resume"
and "who has the coolest personal website?" posts — sorted by likes, plus well-known CS-student portfolios.

The top sites aren't pages, they're **places you explore**:

| Site | Likes | Idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| annacheng8.vercel.app | 442 (#1) | You're in a plane above the clouds; scroll to jump out. Altitude HUD. |
| elliothu.me | 127 | A desktop of objects you can drag, with a dock. |
| nikunjk.com | 45 | Floating 3D rooms connected by bridges, one per section. |
| souravbera.vercel.app | — | A game world: "enter my world", keyboard controls. |
| henryheffernan.com | (famous) | A CS student's portfolio as a full working computer. |
| krishnagupta.web.app | 80 | Local time, a painted banner, handwritten notes. |
| heyamit.dev | 95 | Terminal look, ASCII art, "open to work" sticker. |

The pattern: **one strong concept that's personal**, interactive enough that people play with it, and real details
(local time, live data, your own projects running inside it).

## The concept: VictorOS

A working computer in the browser, set above the clouds — because I'm a cloud architect.

- **The sky is real.** The wallpaper follows London time: dawn, day, dusk or night, with drifting clouds and the skyline.
- **Windows you can drag**, minimise and maximise, with a dock and desktop icons.
- **A terminal with real commands** — `neofetch`, `aws s3 ls` (projects as S3 buckets), `skills`, `certs`, `sudo hire victor`.
- **A browser window** that runs CS Visualiser live inside the site.
- **AWS Console** window with my certifications and the actual architecture of this site, with a request travelling through it.
- **GitHub** window with the live contributions grid and recent repos.
- **hoops.exe** — a basketball game with physics (I play for the Westminster Dragons). Your best score is saved.
- **On phones** it becomes a home screen of the same apps.
- **A plain version** (/plain) for recruiters who want the 30-second read.
